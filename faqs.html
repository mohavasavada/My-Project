Q1: Do you accept international students?
A: Yes, most universities welcome international students and have dedicated offices to assist with visas, language requirements, and cultural support.

Q2: What is campus life like?
A: Campus life is vibrant, with clubs, student organizations, sports teams, cultural events, and student government. It’s a great place to meet people and get involved.

Q3: How much is the tuition?
A: Tuition varies based on program and residency. It can range from a few thousand to tens of thousands as per year.

Q4: Are scholarships or financial aid available?
A: Yes, there are scholarships based on academic merit, need, sports, leadership, or community involvement. International students may also be eligible for some scholarships.

Q5: Do you help students find internships or jobs?
A: Yes, the career center provides help with resumes, interview prep, internships, career fairs, and job placements. Some programs have required internships or co-op placements.
